Course Overview and Purpose

I) Course Descriptor

The Psychology course invites students to discover the principles of psychology as a science while fostering a sense of personal leadership. By exploring historical, chronological, and contextual perspectives, students learn how psychology applies to their individual lives, empowering them to understand their emotions, values, and purpose.

Personal Leadership Focus

Designed to help students Be Proactive by developing self-awareness and emotional intelligence, enabling them to make conscious and constructive choices.

Goal Alignment

Empowers students to Begin with the End in Mind by setting meaningful goals for personal and academic growth throughout the school year.

General Learning Objectives

II) General Objectives

1

Evolving Science & Emotional Well-Being

Understand psychology as a historical and evolving science that improves lives by empowering individuals to take charge of their mental and emotional well-being.

2

Scientific Method & Everyday Challenges

Apply scientific methods used in psychology to everyday challenges, reflecting the habit of Put First Things First.

3

Perspectives on Personality

Explore personality from various psychological and philosophical perspectives to better Seek First to Understand others and themselves.

4

Empathy & Personality Disorders

Learn to recognize and address personality disorders with empathy and professionalism.

5

Mental Health & Holistic Well-Being

Promote mental health and develop emotional intelligence as tools to Sharpen the Saw, ensuring holistic well-being.

Academic Competencies

Subject Specific Core Competencies

Reflect on the conceptual foundations of psychology and its personal and social applications, demonstrating a Win-Win mindset.

Compare and contrast psychological schools of thought within historical, cultural, and social contexts to enhance personal and professional awareness.

Analyze individual differences in personality and apply insights to strengthen interpersonal relationships.

Use psychological principles to nurture responsible and respectful attitudes toward sexuality and life decisions.

Soft Skills & Personal Leadership

Course Development Skills

1. Problem Solving: Develop solutions to case studies, promoting proactive responses.
2. Critical Thinking: Evaluate and compare diverse psychological theories.
3. Creativity: Create engaging presentations that synergize diverse ideas.
4. People Management: Work in groups with defined roles to achieve common goals.
5. Emotional Intelligence: Resolve conflicts using empathy and respect.
6. Judgment & Decision-Making: Align choices with individual values and group goals.
7. Service Orientation: Engage in acts of kindness and support peers.
8. Negotiation: Express ideas and find mutually beneficial solutions.
9. Cognitive Flexibility: Adapt to various learning and teaching methods with openness.

Achievement Indicators & Evaluation Criteria

IV) Achievement Indicators

Reflect on Psychology's Foundations

  • Identify areas of application in psychology and its impact on life and leadership.
  • Recognize mental processes that enhance proactive behaviors.

Analyze Psychological Schools of Thought

  • Relate theories to personal and societal contexts, reinforcing self-awareness and leadership.
  • Apply learned theories in investigations and projects with a proactive approach.

Understand Personality & Relationships

  • Describe emotional traits and personality components that enhance interpersonal synergy.
  • Demonstrate understanding through collaborative activities and personal growth reflections.

Promote Mental Health & Responsible Behaviors

  • Utilize emotional intelligence for positive relationships, demonstrating Think Win-Win.
  • Identify and discuss ethical, cultural, and emotional aspects of life decisions.
  • Cultivate values for future roles in family, community, and professional life.
